FAQ · trust

Answers first

How is this different?

Others tell you “Side Upper”. SwapSeat draws the coach — bay, side, level, walking distance. Flights show exits, wings, lavs.

How does money work?

Maps, listings, requests and chat are free. Trip pass (groups), Boost (highlight, no guarantees), Plus (unlimited, preferences, alerts).

Does it work offline?

Cached maps open offline. Publishing, requests and payments need a connection — an offline tap is never shown as confirmed.
